# Academic Evidence Lane｜学术证据通道

Generate an `academic_evidence_lane` listing which local sources may inform field mapping, their source status, dates, and verification limits.

## Workflow

1. Accept only source classifications with identity evidence.
2. Include source roles, date availability, and reasons for inclusion/exclusion.
3. Keep industry reports, blogs, and discussion material in a separate intelligence lane.
4. State the lane's evidence coverage and missing material.

## Boundaries

This lane supports exploration, not a claim that a source is human-verified or manuscript-eligible. Do not search online.
