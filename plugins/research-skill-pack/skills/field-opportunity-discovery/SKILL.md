# Field Opportunity Discovery｜领域机会发现

Create `field_opportunity_profile` with promising intersections, under-observed contexts, emerging methods, limitations, and evidence coverage. This is the dedicated “领域机会发现” work unit.

## Workflow

1. Read Field Map, Trend Map, Lifecycle, and Growth Momentum with their limits.
2. Identify opportunities only when their supporting sources and counter-considerations are recorded.
3. Distinguish field opportunity from a validated Gap Card.
4. State what additional local evidence is needed before an opportunity can become a research question.

## Boundaries

No invented novelty, no title generation without confirmed context, and no online lookup. Use Low/Medium/High or `insufficient_evidence` only.
