# Research Lifecycle Assessor｜研究生命周期判断

Generate a `research_lifecycle_assessment` such as emerging, expanding, consolidating, mature, or unclear only from dated local evidence and Field Map context.

## Workflow

1. Require source dates and state the observed coverage period.
2. Use transparent narrative indicators rather than a cross-field score.
3. List competing interpretations and missing evidence.
4. Route insufficient date evidence to source import rather than inventing a lifecycle.

## Boundaries

This is an exploratory judgement, not a bibliometric fact or a manuscript claim. `insufficient_evidence` is a valid outcome.
