# Research Trend Mapper｜研究趋势图谱

Create a `research_trend_map` from dated local sources. Keep observed patterns, possible interpretation, and evidence limits distinct.

## Workflow

1. Require a source-linked date for every mapped observation.
2. Map changes in topic, method, population, setting, and evidence type where present.
3. Report gaps in time coverage and source density.
4. Hand numerical window comparison to `$growth-momentum-assessor`.

## Boundaries

Do not claim a current trend from a sparse or undated collection; no live search or forecast is permitted.
