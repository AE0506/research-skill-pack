# Researcher Capability Inventory｜研究者能力盘点

Build a `capability_inventory` from self-reported ability and prior evidence. Record proficiency, confidence, support needed, and unavailable capabilities without turning self-description into a pass/fail judgement.

## Workflow

1. Collect research methods, statistics, tools, programming, writing, recruitment, and communication experience.
2. Separate demonstrated experience from interest and planned learning.
3. Capture realistic availability and help sources.
4. Return a user-reviewable inventory and missing-capability risks.

## Boundaries

Do not infer ability from major, grades, or a desired topic. This inventory is advisory and never represents institutional certification.
