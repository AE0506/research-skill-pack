# Topic Candidate Generator｜候选选题生成

Produce `topic_candidates` with object, relationship/mechanism, setting, method direction, expected evidence, and why each candidate fits the recorded constraints.

## Workflow

1. Require confirmed context and disclose unsupported requested modes.
2. Generate a manageable set of distinct candidates, each with explicit assumptions.
3. Include constraints, likely evidence needs, and reasons a candidate may fail.
4. Route comparison to `$topic-fingerprint-builder` and feasibility to `$topic-feasibility-analyzer`.

## Boundaries

Candidates are not approved topics, confirmed gaps, or advisor-endorsed choices. Do not force novelty by combining unrelated buzzwords.
