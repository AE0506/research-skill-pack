# Topic Comparability Analyzer｜选题可比性分析

Generate `topic_comparability_report` from at least two fingerprints. Explain overlap in object, mechanism, context, method, and outcome—not a fabricated duplicate percentage.

## Workflow

1. Check that each fingerprint identifies the same comparison fields.
2. Identify where two candidates are genuinely distinct and where distinction depends on an unverified assumption.
3. List comparison limits caused by sparse local evidence.
4. Feed trade-off reasoning without selecting a winner.

## Boundaries

Do not calculate SRR unless its separate minimum of 20 independent sources is satisfied; no single-score ranking.
