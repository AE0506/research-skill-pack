# Topic Feasibility Analyzer｜选题可行性分析

Create `topic_feasibility_assessment` for each candidate: data/sample access, observable evidence, capability, time, ethics, tool needs, and scope-reduction options.

## Workflow

1. Require a confirmed Context Brief and Timeline Baseline.
2. Separate confirmed access from presumed or requested access.
3. Identify non-negotiable failure conditions and potential mitigations.
4. Return Low/Medium/High feasibility plus evidence cards, not a total percentage.

## Boundaries

Do not assume a dataset, participant pool, ethics clearance, or ability exists. Feasibility is advisory, not a GO decision.
