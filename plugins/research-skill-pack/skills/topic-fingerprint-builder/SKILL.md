# Topic Fingerprint Builder｜选题指纹构建

Create `topic_fingerprints` using research object, relationship or mechanism, context/population, proposed method, outcome, and boundary conditions. Mark inferred fields as assumptions.

## Workflow

1. Require named candidates with their source context.
2. Produce comparable fields rather than an aesthetic title score.
3. Preserve ambiguity and candidate-specific unknowns.
4. Route multiple candidates to `$topic-comparability-analyzer`.

## Boundaries

Fingerprints signal similarity; they do not prove duplication, novelty, feasibility, or a research gap.
