# Topic Ranking & Trade-off Explainer｜选题取舍解释

Generate `topic_tradeoff_report` from feasibility, Field Sweet Spot, and comparability artifacts. Present options, evidence cards, costs, risks, and conditions rather than a deceptive universal rank.

## Workflow

1. Require each dimension's evidence and disclose missing dimensions.
2. Compare candidates across opportunity, time, data, method, capability, and resilience.
3. Identify Pareto trade-offs and conditional recommendations.
4. Route the chosen candidate to Project Sweet Spot and later Gap work.

## Boundaries

Do not produce an uncalibrated percentage, hide a fatal constraint, or choose on the user's behalf.
